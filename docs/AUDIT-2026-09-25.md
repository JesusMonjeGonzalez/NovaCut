## Remaining release gates

1. Audit Cargo.lock against current advisories; review upgrades rather than blindly bumping majors.
2. Pin the FFmpeg download to an approved immutable version and embedded SHA-256.
   Currently both the ZIP and checksum come from mutable upstream URLs.
3. Make FFmpeg replacement transactional; a failed update can partially replace existing tools.
4. Fix and regression-test FFmpeg LUT path escaping for apostrophes and filter metacharacters.
5. Re-run macOS 14 and Windows CI on the final commit, including PowerShell packaging tests.
6. Validate exact packaged artifacts on clean Windows and macOS systems, including Intel,
   installation, upgrade, uninstall, launch, export and preservation of user projects.
7. Review the complete third-party distribution inventory. Including existing notices alone
   is not a complete licensing review.
8. Obtain signing/notarization credentials if authenticated publisher distribution is required.
   Ad-hoc signing and checksums do not authenticate the publisher.
9. With owner approval, commit/push corrections, merge PR #5 after checks, and publish a
   version-matched tag via the release workflow. Verify uploaded assets and checksums afterwards.

This is a bounded engineering review, not a certification that the project is free
of secrets, vulnerabilities or runtime defects. The corrections were published as
the v0.2.0 prerelease on 2026-09-25 from merge commit a9170e8, after Windows,
macOS 14, secret-scan and package-verification jobs passed on the tagged commit.
